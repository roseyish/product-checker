# Northloom Sustainability Report FY2025 (fictional sample brand)

## Materials
In FY2025, 62% of the polyester used across our outerwear range was recycled polyester certified to the Global Recycled Standard (scope certificate GRS-2025-04471, issued by an accredited certification body). The remaining 38% was virgin polyester. Across all product categories, recycled polyester represented 41% of total polyester purchased by weight.

All cotton products are made with cotton sourced through the Better Cotton programme. Better Cotton uses a mass balance chain of custody and does not guarantee that any individual garment physically contains Better Cotton.

Our organic cotton T-shirt line (12 styles) is certified to the Global Organic Textile Standard, licence number GOTS-NL-88213. Organic cotton represented 9% of our total cotton purchases in FY2025.

## Climate
Our headquarters and two owned warehouses achieved carbon neutrality for scope 1 and scope 2 emissions in FY2025 through a combination of a 31% reduction against a FY2019 baseline and the purchase of verified carbon credits covering the remaining 6,400 tonnes CO2e. Scope 3 emissions, which represent 94% of our total footprint, are not included in this carbon-neutral boundary. We have not set a validated science-based target for scope 3.

## Water and chemicals
Our two largest denim suppliers, representing 58% of denim volume, are bluesign system partners. Water consumption per pair of jeans at these two suppliers fell 22% between FY2022 and FY2025. We do not currently measure water use at our other denim suppliers.

## Circularity
We launched a take-back programme in three countries in FY2025 and collected 18,200 garments. Of these, 61% were resold, 27% were downcycled into insulation material, and 12% were sent to energy recovery. No garments collected through the programme were fibre-to-fibre recycled into new Northloom products.

## Packaging
All e-commerce mailer bags are made from 100% post-consumer recycled LDPE, verified by supplier declarations and an independent audit in March 2025. Garment polybags remain virgin LDPE.

# Veridian Apparel Impact Statement 2025 (fictional sample brand)

## Fibres
Veridian sourced 100% of its wool from farms certified to the Responsible Wool Standard in 2025 (scope certificate RWS-2025-1190). Wool made up 34% of total fibre volume.

Recycled content across the total collection reached 28% by weight, up from 19% in 2023. The "ReLoop" capsule collection (6 styles) is made from 100% recycled nylon reclaimed from fishing nets, verified under the Global Recycled Standard, licence GRS-2025-07732.

## Carbon
Veridian has committed to a 50% absolute reduction in scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions by 2030 from a 2022 baseline. In 2025 total emissions were 4% below the 2022 baseline. Veridian does not purchase offsets and does not describe any product as carbon neutral.

## Labour
All tier 1 factories (100%, 41 sites) were audited against our Code of Conduct in 2025. 78% of workers in tier 1 factories were paid at or above a living wage benchmark; 22% were paid the legal minimum wage but below the benchmark.

## Chemicals
Veridian is not a bluesign system partner and does not hold OEKO-TEX certification for any product. All suppliers are contractually required to comply with our Restricted Substances List, tested annually.
